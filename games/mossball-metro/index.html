How To Play

  • Objects stick only when they are smaller than your mossball's current grip size.
  • Collect in quick chains for route bonuses and time crumbs.
  • Cleaning drones scrape moss away. Tram sweepers are slower but hit hard.
  • Hit the quota on all three platforms to clear the full metro route.

Route Notes

Start a run, then follow the warm pollen lanes toward small objects first. Larger props become collectible as the ball grows.
