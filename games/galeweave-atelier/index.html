Atelier Method

  • Torque is weight × distance from the pivot. Equal left and right torque unlocks the test.
  • Sailier glass catches more wind. Low wind bias makes the live test calmer and earns a bonus.
  • During the test, hold A/D, arrow keys, or the touch buttons to slide the brass counterweight.
  • Keep the beam near level until the clock expires. Stability recovers whenever the mobile settles.
